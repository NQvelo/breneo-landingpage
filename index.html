Frequently asked questions

What is Breneo?

Breneo is an AI-powered career orientation and upskilling platform that helps you discover your strengths, match with ideal careers, and prepare for real-world opportunities.

How does the AI-powered test work?

Breneo’s dynamic test evaluates your interests, technical skills, and soft skills. The AI adapts the questions based on your answers to provide a personalized assessment of your potential.

Do I need any prior experience or skills to use Breneo?

No. Breneo is designed for everyone - whether you are a student, a recent graduate, or a professional looking to grow.

How accurate are the career recommendations?

Breneo’s recommendations are based on your test results and advanced AI analysis, providing matches tailored to your strengths, interests, and skill set.

Can Breneo help me get real job opportunities?

Yes. Pro users receive suggestions for jobs and internships that match their skills and readiness for the job market.

Can I retake the test to update my results?

Yes. You can retake the assessment anytime to reflect new skills, interests, or career goals.

What is the difference between the Free and Pro plans?

The Free Plan gives you access to the basic AI assessment and limited career insights. Breneo Pro unlocks the full dynamic assessment, personalized learning paths, advanced career matches, skill tracking, and job recommendations

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade from Basic to Pro anytime directly from your account settings. It takes just a few clicks.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your plan anytime. Your access will stay active until the end of your billing period.
