Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, improve, and personalize your experience on Breneo. The types of data we collect include:
a. Personal Information
When you register or use our services, we may collect:
  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Account credentials (password is securely encrypted)
  • Educational background or professional experience (if provided)
b. Usage Data
We automatically collect information on how you interact with Breneo, such as:
  • Pages you visit
  • Time spent on pages
  • Device type and browser
  • IP address and approximate location
c. Learning and Assessment Data
When you take tests, complete courses, or interact with AI recommendations, we may collect:
  • Test answers and results
  • Learning progress and completion status
  • Skills and career preferences inferred from your activity
d. Communication Data
If you contact us, we collect:
  • Your messages, feedback, or support requests
  • Email communication metadata (for customer support purposes)
2. How We Use Your Information
We use collected information to:
  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Personalize learning paths and job recommendations
  • Analyze user progress and platform performance
  • Improve Breneo’s AI models and testing systems
  • Send important updates, course notifications, or account alerts
  • Ensure safety, security, and compliance with legal requirements
We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We may share limited information with:
  • Trusted partners and academies — when you enroll in a course provided by an academy through Breneo.
  • Service providers — who help us host the platform, process data, or send communications (e.g., Supabase, Render, or similar platforms).
  • Legal authorities — only if required by law or to protect Breneo’s rights and users’ safety.
All partners are required to handle your data securely and in accordance with applicable privacy laws.
4. Data Retention
We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services.
You can request data deletion at any time by contacting us at [email protected].
5. Your Rights
You have the right to:
  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Withdraw consent for certain data uses
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
To exercise these rights, contact us at [email protected].
6. Security
We use encryption, secure connections (HTTPS), and data protection practices to keep your information safe.
However, please note that no online service is completely risk-free, so we encourage you to protect your login credentials.
7. Cookies and Tracking
Breneo uses cookies and similar technologies to:
  • Keep you signed in
  • Remember preferences
  • Analyze usage for platform improvement
You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may limit some features.
8. Third-Party Links
Our platform may contain links to third-party academies, job portals, or external services.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. Please review their privacy policies separately.
9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be announced on our website or sent via email.
